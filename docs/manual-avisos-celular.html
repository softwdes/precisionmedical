Turn on alerts

Same on both phones — open the installed app first

  1. 1

    In the top bar, find the phone icon.

    Next to Messages. It is a phone, not a bell — the bell is system news.

  2. 2

    Tap it. A short note explains what the alert will say.

  3. 3

    Tap Turn on.

  4. 4

    Your phone asks for permission. Tap Allow.

    This appears once. If you tap Block, only your phone settings can undo it.

  5. 5

    The icon turns green. That is the whole confirmation.

Lock screen

It rings once and stays there until you read it. Tap it and the app opens on that message.

The alert only names who wrote to you. Never the subject, the patient, or the reason — anyone glancing at your screen learns nothing.

If alerts stop coming

The icon says green but nothing arrives

This happens after the app is uninstalled and installed again. The fix is one tap:

  1. 1

    Tap the green phone icon.

  2. 2

    Tap Reactivate alerts.

Send yourself a test message afterwards to confirm.

Samsung phones need one extra check. Samsung puts apps to sleep on its own, and a sleeping app cannot wake up for an alert. Open Settings › Battery › Background usage limits and make sure the app is not in Sleeping apps or Deep sleeping apps.

Stuck on any of these steps? Send a message from the desktop app and someone will walk you through it.
